My previous articles on meese.rs were heavily drafted with the assistance of AI, which is a
fantastic assistant due the ability to search through all my Git history, chat sessions, and notes
in a short period of time. I was of the school of thought that while AI _could_ write content on
anything, it requires a skilled author with knowledge on the subject matter to produce anything of
value. The insertion point of "taste" still remained, which is not yet something that machines can
produce without proper guidance.

However, reading
"[Why You Should Almost Never Use AI to Write Anything Substantive](https://www.erichgrunewald.com/posts/why-i-think-you-should-almost-never-use-ai-to-write-anything-substantive/)"
by Erich Grunewald has shifted my mentality regarding the publishing of primarily AI-generated
content. For future posts I may consider a general strategy suggested by AI, but the writing will be
done by myself. This will produce significantly less volume, but will hopefully imbue more value
into each word.
